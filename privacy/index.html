This policy explains how AI Media Studio (“AMS”, “we”, “our”) accesses, uses, stores and protects information when an authorized user connects a Google or YouTube account.

AI Media Studio does not ask users for their Google or YouTube password. Access is granted through Google OAuth and can be revoked by the user.

1. YouTube API Services

AI Media Studio uses YouTube API Services. Users of these features remain subject to Google’s privacy practices and YouTube’s applicable terms.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

2. Data we access

Depending on the permissions granted, AMS may access:

  • the identity of the authorized YouTube channel;
  • video metadata needed to prepare or verify an authorized upload;
  • YouTube Analytics data for authorized videos, including views, engaged views and average view duration;
  • OAuth authorization tokens required to maintain the authorized connection.

AMS does not request, collect or store a user’s Google or YouTube password.

3. How the data is used

Authorized data is used only to operate the media workflow requested by the authorized user: verifying the connected channel, performing operator-approved YouTube actions, collecting first-party performance analytics, and recording the resulting learning and audit evidence.

AMS does not sell authorized Google or YouTube user data, use it for advertising, or use it for unrelated secondary purposes.

4. Service providers

AMS may use infrastructure providers acting on its behalf, including n8n for workflow automation and OAuth credential handling, Supabase for application records, and Vercel for public website hosting. These providers are used only as necessary to operate the service.

5. Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored in the configured automation credential store and are not displayed publicly. Analytics and operational records are retained only for legitimate operational, audit and learning purposes.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

Users can revoke AI Media Studio’s Google account access at any time from Google security settings.

A user may also request deletion of data stored by AMS by emailing lucas3001bondi@gmail.com. AMS will process valid deletion requests in accordance with applicable Google and YouTube requirements.

Deleting data held by AMS does not delete content or data held by YouTube itself.

7. Security

AMS uses access controls, encrypted transport, scoped OAuth permissions and operational separation intended to protect credentials and authorized data from unauthorized access.

8. Cookies and tracking

This public informational website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party behavioral tracking. Hosting providers may process standard request metadata for security and delivery purposes.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when AMS functionality or applicable requirements change. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests: lucas3001bondi@gmail.com.
